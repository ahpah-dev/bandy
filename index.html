FAQ

Good questions.

Is Bandy really free?

Yes. There are no paid tiers, ads or limits. The code is public domain, so it will stay free.

Do I need an account?

Not to make music. Your projects are saved in your browser automatically. An account (just an email link, no password) is only needed to upload songs to the Song browser.

Where are my projects stored?

In your browser’s own storage on your device – nothing is sent anywhere. Export a .bandy file to back up or move a project.

Can I record with a microphone or MIDI keyboard?

Yes. Allow microphone access to record audio, and plug in a MIDI keyboard to play instruments (Chrome and Edge support Web MIDI).

How do uploads work?

Export your song as WAV (or any MP3/OGG/M4A/FLAC file you have), then upload it in the Song browser. Songs are public, limited to 20 MB, and you must own the rights. Anyone can report a song and reported songs are hidden for review.

Is Bandy affiliated with BandLab?

No. Bandy is an independent project inspired by browser-based studios. It contains none of their code, assets or sounds.
